Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is a Mali virtual phone number?

    A Mali virtual phone number is a digital phone number that follows the local Mali number format but isn't tied to any physical phone line. Therefore, you can access your Mali virtual number from anywhere in the world via a high-speed internet connection.

  • Where can I forward calls made to my Mali phone number?

    You can forward calls made to your Mali phone number to any existing phone line that you have control of. This include mobile, business, and personal lines. You could also forward calls to a call center.
