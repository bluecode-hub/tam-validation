We’re here to help

Our carrier device financing platform opens new revenue generation opportunities for telecom carriers in underserved markets, including prepaid plans, reducing the risks of bad debt and missed customer payments.

Benefits

Say ‘yes’ to more customers with carrier device financing

‘Nudge’ consumers into paying upcoming and outstanding bills with Trustonic. On average, Trustonic helps reduce delinquency and bad debt, in prepaid and postpaid, financed smartphones by 70%. Typically, that allows you to say ‘yes’ to 100% more customers. Our telco financing solution ensures more of your device financing customers pay on time and gives you the tools to manage those that don’t. Increase smartphone affordability and grow your revenues, without increasing commercial risk.

Drive Premium Sales, Boost ARPU

Give your customers access to better devices they never thought they could afford. With prepaid and postpaid programs, 4G smartphones become affordable, helping millions upgrade from basic 2G handsets to feature-rich 4G devices. That means bigger mid-tier and premium smartphone sales volumes, higher ARPU for carriers, and new revenue streams unlocked across untapped markets.

Improve revenue assurance for subsidized smartphones

Want to ensure only your SIM is used in subsidized smartphones during the subsidy period? With Trustonic, you can. If a customer uses a different SIM card in the subsidized device you can restrict access to key features. Ultimately, you can lock the device. Protect your investment while providing peace of mind as you grow your customer base.

Supply chain device theft could become a thing of the past

Protect smartphones from factory to recycling with Trustonic. Our platform fully locks down stolen smartphones worldwide, even in countries without IMEI blacklisting. When a device is stolen, our technology displays a locked screen, rendering it useless and eliminating resale value. Say ‘No’ to smartphone theft and fraud with Trustonic – safeguarding margins and profits.

Reduce your operational expenditure

Increase productivity and profitability for your business. Our MNO device financing solution allows you to reassign up to 50% of your call center collection staff to revenue-generating activities. By automating the collections process, you won’t need to allocate headcount, or collection agency budget, to chase payments. Our handset financing solution streamlines your operations, optimizing your resources to focus on revenue generation.
