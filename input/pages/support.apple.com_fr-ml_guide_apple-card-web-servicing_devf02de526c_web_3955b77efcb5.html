    View Apple Card Monthly Installments online

    When you purchase an eligible Apple product, you can choose Apple Card Monthly Installments as your payment type at checkout instead of choosing to pay in full. For more information, see the Apple Card Monthly Installments website and the Apple Support article Products you can buy with Apple Card Monthly Installments.

    You can view details about your Apple Card Monthly Installments in a supported web browser.

    View Apple Card Monthly Installments

    You can view the monthly installment details of each device that you purchased with Apple Card Monthly Installments.

    1. At card.apple.com, click Installments in the sidebar.

      Note: If you haven’t made a purchase with Apple Card Monthly Installments, you won’t see the Installments page.

    2. Click the device that you want to see more details about.

      • If you have multiple devices: Devices with current installments due are listed at the top of the page, with the most recently purchased item at the top. Devices you finished paying off appear below Complete.

      • If you only have one device: The details are already visible.

    3. Do any of the following:

      • See progress overview: A bar graph at the top of the page shows the total amount paid.

      • Review installment details: Click View Details.

      • View installment history: Below Monthly Installment History, you can see a history of your installments with the most recent at the top.

    Make an early installment payment

    If you already paid your Apple Card balance, you can make an early payment toward your Apple Card Monthly Installments.

    1. At card.apple.com, click Payments in the sidebar.

    2. Click Pay Installments Early at the bottom of the page.

    3. Enter the amount you want to pay, select a payment date, then do one of the following:

      • If you select today’s date: Click Pay Now.

      • If you select a date in the future: Click Pay Later.

    4. If you added multiple bank accounts, select the one you want to use.

    5. Click the Pay button again.

    If you have an iPhone or iPad, you can view more details about your installments in Wallet. See the Apple Support article How to view and pay Apple Card Monthly Installments.

    To access and use all Apple Card features and products available only to Apple Card users, you must add Apple Card to Wallet on an iPhone or iPad that supports and has the latest version of iOS or iPadOS. Apple Card is subject to credit approval, available only for qualifying applicants in the United States, and issued by Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Salt Lake City Branch. If you reside in the U.S. territories, please call Goldman Sachs at 877-255-5923 with questions about Apple Card.
