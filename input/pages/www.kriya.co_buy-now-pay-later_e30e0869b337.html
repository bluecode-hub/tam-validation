How PayLater works

1.

Buyer places order on your website or offline sales channel.
  • We check new buyers and give a credit limit in seconds.
  • Buyers choose to pay in 30, 60 or 90 days, or in 3 monthly instalments.
  • We pay you upfront.
  • You fulfil the order as normal.

2.

Get paid immediately.

3.

We take care of collecting payment.
  • Your buyer pays us on their terms with their choice of bank transfer, direct debit or open banking.
  • We handle collections and reconciliation for you.
