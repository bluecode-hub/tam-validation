Mobile payment

Replace your wallet with your mobile phone, which now allows you to perform different types of payments.

Why use mobile payment?

Swap your wallet for your phone

Start paying with your mobile phone, which is always at your hand.

Service availability

You can choose mobile payment at any store offering contactless payment.

Easy, quick, and safe

Mobile payment is quick, easy, and completely safe.

You may choose between different options

Your mobile phone allows you to use different options of mobile payment.

Google Pay

Google Pay is intended for Android smartphone users and replaces the classic wallet.
  • Pay with your Android phone, which you always have with you.
  • You can pay with mobile at any store offering contactless payments.
  • You do not pay any fees for using the service.
Learn More

Apple Pay

Forget your wallet. Pay with you iPhone.
  • Start paying with your iPhone, which is always at your hand.
  • You can pay with mobile at any store offering contactless payments
  • You do not pay any fees for using the service.
Learn More

Flik immediate payments

Quick and easy payments to your contacts who are customers of our bank, 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
  • Just by using a phone number or email address.
  • Instant transfer of funds and sending request for payment.
  • It works 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
Learn More

Flik immediate payments

Flik payment is an immediate payment with a mobile phone, which can be performed at any time.
  • The service works between Flik users of all Slovenian banks.
  • Start paying with your smart phone, which is always at your hand.
  • Pay using a mobile number or e-mail address, free of charge.
