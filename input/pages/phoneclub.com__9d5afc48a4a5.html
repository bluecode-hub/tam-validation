FAQs about international calling with PhoneClub.com

  • How to make international calls with PhoneClub.com?
    Making international calls with PhoneClub.com is simple. Use our PhoneClub app or dial an access number from any phone.
    • Using the PhoneClub app: Download our free app from the App Store or Google Play Store, create an account or log in, buy Voice Credit, and call directly from your contacts or dial the number in international format (destination country code and phone number). No access numbers needed.
    • Using an access number: Dial an access number in your area, enter your PIN or activate PINless, and dial the destination number in international format. This method works from any mobile or landline phone.
  • How much does it cost to make international calls with PhoneClub.com? Are there different types of plans?
    The cost of international calls varies depending on the country you are calling. We are committed to transparent and competitive pricing without hidden fees. You can easily check the exact per-minute rates for any country on our dedicated international calling rates page on our website or within the PhoneClub mobile app before making a call.
    We offer a range of flexible options to suit different calling needs:
    • Voice Credit: You add funds to your account and pay per minute based on the destination country. This offers flexibility without a fixed commitment.
    • Monthly Plans: These subscriptions offer a set amount of calling minutes to specific countries for a recurring monthly fee, providing better rates for frequent callers. For a selection of countries we also offer plans with unlimited minutes, that can be found on our dedicated international calling plans page.
    • World Unlimited Plan: With this plan you can call 50+ countries and it is the ideal choice for frequent calls to several countries, offering the best rate. Check the countries included in the plan here.
  • Does PhoneClub.com have an app for international calling?
    Yes, PhoneClub.com has a dedicated international calling app: PhoneClub App. You can download it for free from the App Store for iOS and the Google Play Store for Android. It's the most convenient way to make international calls straight from your contacts list, check rates, manage your balance, and more.
  • How do I add balance to my account for international calling?
    You can easily add balance to your PhoneClub.com account for international calling through several methods:
    • PhoneClub Mobile App: log in, navigate from the hamburger menu to "Buy Calling Credit", select the desired amount and pay via the available payment methods. (This option depends on the mobile operating system used.)
    • On Our Website: log in to your account, click on "Buy Voice Credit" or select a calling plan, and follow the on-screen instructions to add balance using a credit/debit card, PayPal, or other available payment methods.
  • What are the advantages of using PhoneClub.com compared to traditional calling cards?
    Using PhoneClub.com for international calls offers key advantages over calling cards:
    • Convenience: Make calls directly from your smartphone using our app or from any phone using an access number.
    • Transparent Pricing: See the exact per-minute rate before you call, with no hidden fees or surprise charges common with some calling cards.
    • Easy Recharge: Add funds instantly through our app or website anytime, anywhere.
    • Enhanced Features: Our app provides features like contact integration, call history, and easy balance checking.
    • Better Value: Our transparent pricing and competitive rates can be more cost-effective, especially for frequent callers.
