Frequently asked Questions

  • What is the best eSim for Mali?

    Blink eSIM offers you many Internet eSIM plan options with the most reliable connection backed by local operators like Societe des telecommunications du Mali (SOTELMA) | ORANGE MALI SA

  • How much does it cost to use BLINK eSIM in Mali

    In Mali BLINK eSIM works on a per GB pricing model since flat-rate internet is not available. When you arrive, you'll receive an SMS with the internet rate $9.6 per GB. Simply follow the link in the SMS to enable internet access. Your wallet will be charged based on the data you use, and once 100% of the purchased data is consumed, the next top-up will be applied automatically at the same rate.

  • Can I use BLINK eSIM for calls and texts in Mali

    BLINK eSIM is designed for internet/data usage only. It does not support traditional voice calls or SMS/text messages. However, you can use popular apps like WhatsApp, FaceTime, Telegram, or Skype to make calls and send messages over the internet as long as your data is active.

  • What should I do if I encounter connectivity issues with BLINK eSIM in Mali

    If you're facing connectivity issues while using BLINK eSIM, don’t worry - we’re here to help! First, make sure your eSIM is installed, active, and mobile data is turned on. Also try toggling airplane mode or restarting your phone. If the issue continues, please contact our support team on WhatsApp or email us at [email protected]. Our team is available 24/7 to assist you.

  • What networks does BLINK eSIM use in Mali

    BLINK eSIM connects to top local networks, we currently use Societe des telecommunications du Mali (SOTELMA) | ORANGE MALI SA. Your eSIM will automatically connect to the strongest available network. If you ever face any issues, feel free to reach out to our support team via WhatsApp or email us at [email protected]. We're here to help!

  • Will I get high-speed internet with BLINK eSIM in Mali

    Yes! With BLINK eSIM, you get high-speed internet under our simple daily flat rate of $3.5/day. Each day, high-speed data is automatically activated just after midnight. If you use up your high-speed data during the day, you’ll still stay connected at reduced speeds - or you can choose to instantly restore high-speed internet by clicking a special link we send via SMS or WhatsApp. This will apply an additional $3.5 charge from your wallet. Our fair usage policy ensures everyone gets a smooth experience while traveling.

  • Is Blink eSIM cheaper than buying a SIM card in Mali

    In most cases, yes! BLINK eSIM offers a flat rate of just $3.5 per day, with no hidden fees, roaming charges, or the hassle of finding and buying a local SIM. You can install and activate it before your trip and get connected instantly upon landing - saving you both time and money. Plus, with one eSIM that works in multiple countries at the same rate, it’s a cost-effective and convenient travel companion.

  • Is BLINK eSIM compatible with phones sold in Mali

    Yes, BLINK eSIM is compatible with most unlocked phones sold that support eSIM technology. Please ensure your phone is eSIM-enabled and unlocked to use it with BLINK eSIM. You can also refer to our compatible devices list for more details.
