Mastercard Installments

Installments made simple, shopping made smarter

Mastercard Installments can be added as a feature of our debit and credit cards, offering accessible installment options with an enhanced consumer experience.

 

1 in 4

consumers have used some form of Buy Now, Pay Later[1]

43%

of consumers would delay a purchase or opt for a cheaper product if BNPL were not available[2]

70%

of users prefer to use BNPL offers from their banks[3]

MASTERCARD INSTALLMENTS

A trusted, convenient way to pay

Mastercard Installments lets consumers shop now and pay later, buying what they need and spreading payments over time.

Omni-channel acceptance

Enable installments on domestic, cross-border, online and in-store transactions.

Customizable installment offers

Define installment tenures and repayment options to deliver customer-centric offers.

Security and peace of mind

Offer consumers a payment experience built on trusted infrastructure, safeguarded by Mastercard's data and security standards.

Seamless user experience

Engage consumers with a digital-centric experience to choose the right payment plan.

Merchants can increase basket size and grow sales

Merchants enjoy peace of mind knowing they always get paid in full and all installment transactions are safeguarded by Mastercard standards and protections.

Issuers and lenders can drive consumer preference with installment solutions

Tailor-made installment offers to serve various customer needs, driving top of wallet behavior and tapping into new revenue streams.

Consumers benefit from enhanced convenience, choice and security

Whether with an existing card or a new line of credit, consumers can pay in installments across millions of merchants.

Give consumers more payment options

Buy Now Pay Later solutions

Pre-approved offers in lender's mobile app which can be accessed via an installment credential. Acceptance across the Mastercard network online and in-store.

Card-linked installments

Mastercard Installments Payment Services enables issuers to add installments as a feature on their existing card portfolios. Consumers can choose to pay in installments using their existing card, both during purchase and post purchase.

This website uses cookies and similar technologies, which may include pixels tags, SDKs and web beacons (collectively ‘Cookies’). We use first and third-party Cookies to operate and improve our sites, measure our audience, enhance the user experience and provide users with content and advertising tailored to their interests based on their browsing activities and actions on the site and other sites/apps.
