    All Jazz users can now apply for the handsets on financing

    Customer will be notified regarding the approval/rejection of the request via SMS

    Customer requests may be declined under the following circumstances:

    • If the applicant is listed as a defaulter with CIB (Credit Information Bureau).
    • When the applicant's CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card) is expired.
    • If the provided references by the applicant do not respond to verification calls.
    • Requests will not be approved if there are outstanding payments indicated on the electricity bill.
    • Applications will be rejected if the salary range or bank amount shown does not meet the criteria for finance capping.

    • Salary (Amount)
    • Salary Slip or Bank Statement of last month

    • Two References (Name / Contact Number / Relation)

    The following are the policy, coverage and redemption process details for Takaful Coverage offered as a part of your Smartphone/Device purchase from Kistpay Private Limited on Financing.

    Scope of Cover

    Smartphones, Tablets & other similar devices Takaful coverage covers the loss resulting / arising out of theft due to violent means / or armed hold up

    Insurance Policy Details

    1. The customer is required to call CPLC helpline on (021) 35682222 and report the stolen handset immediately. The CPLC provides the complaint number which the customer has to email/WhatsApp to Kistpay
    2. The customer calls Kistpay helpline on 111-111-222 to report the stolen phone and provides the complaint number
    3. The customer has to get the FIR registered for claim processing and provide the following documents:
      • Completed Takaful Claim form signed and thumb-printed (to be provided)
      • CNIC Copy
      • Police Report
      • Warranty Card
      • Sales Invoice/Receipt
      • Evidence of Finance
      • Complete accessories (including original box)
      • Customer to carefully retain all warranty cards, invoices and accessories

    Upon submission of all documents the insurance company processes claim and settles payment to Kistpay Private Limited within 2 weeks.

    In case of any request for benefit, loss will be settled on the depreciated sum covered or the market value of the mobile phone at the time of loss, whichever is lesser.

    Once Subscribed, coverage is non-cancellable under the scheme.

    Exclusions to the Policy

    Insurance does not cover:

    • Mysterious and unexplainable Disappearance
    • Unverifiable accidents
    • Pick pocketing
    • Willful negligence
    • Theft from unattended vehicle
    • Data Loss and software malfunction
    • Consequential loss and legal liability of all description
    • Design defects, perils ideally covered under warranty, and manufacturers defects
    • War, Civil War
    • Nuclear, Chemical and Biological risk exclusion
    • The Risk of Accidental Damage, Terrorism and Sabotage whilst being the responsibility of the Proposer/Affinity Partner/Courier Service.
    • Loss of mobile/s due to the involvement of any employee's and/or security guard of the proposer or the courier service.
