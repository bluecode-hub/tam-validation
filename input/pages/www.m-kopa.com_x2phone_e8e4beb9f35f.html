Sim Lock Policy

Your X2 smartphone is secured with a SIM-lock feature. This means:

The phone is designed to work exclusively with your registered SIM card.

If a different SIM card is inserted, the phone will automatically lock.

If the phone is lost or stolen, unauthorized use of another SIM card will trigger a lock.

Your registered SIM card must always remain in the device for it to function properly.

For any SIM card or phone number changes, visit your nearest M-KOPA shop with your identification document for support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of getting the M-KOPA X2?
The M-KOPA X2 is a part of the X-series family. The M-KOPA X2 phone is ‘More than a phone’, giving you access to add-ons such as free 4GB Safaricom data, medical insurance, and phone protection for broken screens or in case of theft. You can also unlock access to our embedded financial services like cash loans.
How do I make payments on the M-KOPA X2?
The payments you make on the M-KOPA X2 Phone can be done by simply using the M-KOPA mobile app that is already on the M-KOPA X2 phone. Here’s a quick guide.
1. Open the M-KOPA app
2. Tap ‘Make Payment’ and follow the prompts.
An alternative to making payments on the M-KOPA X2 is through our official Pay Bill 333222.  Note: M-KOPA does not accept any cash payments.
How do I unlock the M-KOPA X2 after making payments?
Ensure you have your Wi-Fi or mobile data with an active data bundle on your SIM card. Once your Wi-Fi or mobile data is turned on and connected, you can unlock your M-KOPA X2 Phone.
Are there software updates available on the M-KOPA X2?
Yes, there are. Regular software updates will be available on the M-KOPA X2 phone. Follow these simple steps to check for updates:
1. Go to Settings
2. Tap ‘System’
3. Go to ‘System update’
If a software update is available, kindly proceed to update the M-KOPA X2 phone.
What are the benefits of the free health insurance cover that comes with the M-KOPA X2?
If you are admitted to receive treatments in any hospital registered by the Ministry of Health for one night or more, you will receive a cashback of Sh.1,000 per night of admission. This M-KOPA health insurance benefit is available only for a maximum of 30 nights per year, with a yearly limit of Sh. 30,000.
Does this M-KOPA insurance cover family members?
This M-KOPA health insurance benefit only covers the person whose information was registered, when purchasing the M-KOPA X2 Phone.
How do I claim for the M-KOPA X2 insurance in-patient cover?
To claim your in-patient cover, call us on 0800 221 245 or text Turaco on WhatsApp via 0768387 245 as soon as the incident occurs (within a maximum of one week), and one of our agents will assist you with the necessary documentation.
How long does it take to get a claim paid? And how will it be received?
In-patient claims are disbursed to you within 3 business days of receiving full documentation. The money will be sent to your M-PESA or via Airtel Money.
What if I can't afford hospital bills at the time of hospitalisation? Can I qualify for cash upfront?
Hospital cash doesn’t provide upfront cash to members. They must first clear their medical bills and file a claim with Turaco. Once approved, Turaco will pay their claim within 3 days.
What is the device protection benefit that comes with the M-KOPA X2?
Affordable access to one (1) screen repair at M-KOPA authorised outlets for KES 1000 during the first year of purchase.
What do you do when your device is stolen?
M-KOPA customers can access an affordable replacement of a stolen X2 phone for KES 3000 during the first year of purchase.
My phone was stolen, what is the M-KOPA process for replacement?
During this period, if a customer’s device is stolen, they can visit an M-KOPA-authorised service centre to file a claim and pay KES 3000 for a replacement phone.
How do I qualify for a cash loan?
After three months of on-time repayments, customers are eligible for access to mobile cash loans during their phone loan repayment period.
Can I make a claim for any of the device protection benefits immediately after purchasing the M-KOPA X2?
You only have a one-month waiting period from when the M-KOPA X2 Phone is purchased, before you can make a claim. If your phone gets stolen, you can put in a claim of KES 3000 to get a replacement phone. Also, as an M-KOPA customer, you get your broken screen replaced by paying just KES 1000.
Is the device protection benefit free for me?
To access the M-KOPA X2 phone protection benefit, you are expected to pay the copay upfront before a successful benefit claim is logged. An upfront amount of KES 3,000 is paid for lost or stolen phones and a co-pay of KES 1,000 is paid for broken screen repair.
What is required for me to make a claim for the M-KOPA X2 broken screen repair?
So long as you have not made claims for the M-KOPA X2 Phone broken screen repair, you are expected to make a co-pay payment of KES 1,000. Once this is done, you are required to present the phone with the broken screen, a valid ID, and fill out an indemnification form.
What is required for me to make a claim for a stolen M-KOPA X2 replacement?
So long as you have not made claims for replacement prior to your request, you are required to make a co-pay payment of KES 3,000, present a stamped police report or OB,  a valid ID, and fill out a claim form.
How long should I wait to have a replacement of a stolen M-KOPA X2 or have a broken screen repaired?
The turnaround time is as follows:
1. Broken screen repair: 10 days
2. Lost/Stolen replacement: 48 hours
How do I maintain access to all these X2 benefits?
You would need to stick to your repayment plan, make payments and always have your payment statuses on GREEN on the M-KOPA App.
