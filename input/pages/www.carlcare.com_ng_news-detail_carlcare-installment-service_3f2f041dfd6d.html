Carlcare Offers Installment Service with ₦0 down Payment Now

2022-06-30
Jide Mbaka

If there is something wrong with your mobile phone, but you don't have enough maintenance budget at the moment. Well. Carlcare is here to help! Recently, Carlcare has released launched a new service - installment service with EdgeCross, to help you out with this kind of budget dilemma. EdgeCross is a credit installment product in partnership with Carlcare.

Why Choose EdgeCross?

1)Low threshold, unsecured

  • You only need a mobile + BVN account to get a mortgage-free service

2)One application, high circulation

  • Up to 100,000 Naira quotas for one application
  • Within the scope of the quota, multiple loans can be used for recycling

3)Quick application, fast loan

  • Three-step application on the whole line
  • Real-time approval, the fastest 5 minutes loan

4)Flexible cycle and convenient payment

  • Flexible loan period to meet various consumer demand
  • Support multiple repayment methods: active repayment and automatic repayment

How to Register and Login for the Installment Service?

If you are interested in this service, please update your Carlcare App to the latest version. 

1. Login and register:

Click banner on carlcare.

2. Authorize:

After the pop-up authorization dialog box, click “agree”go to Edgecross page.

For repair problems, please contact Carlcare customer service. For financial loan and repayment problems, please contact us in the following ways. EdgeCross telephone: 018888188

Application Process for Installment Service

1. Personal information

  • Fill your true information：gender、Education、Marrid status.
  • Fill in 2 contacts:1 family member, 1 friend

2. BVN verification &complet account

Verify your identity through the BVN information provided

  • BVN number
  • Name (first name, middle name, family name)
  • Disburse money to the account you provided
  • account ：Name of Bank, Bank accout number

3. Add bank card

  • Fill in the bank card information: Bank card number /expiration date
  • Enter the PIN or Verification code to Verify it's your card.

4. Submit

  • Click “OK” for applying for credit.

5. Withdraw loan

  • When you get the limit, the page will show : Available limit.
  • Click GO to enter the carlcare product installment page.
  • Select the item you want to installment.
  • Select the period of installment.（currently only 4 Tenor with 14 days per tenor）
  • Click OK to submit the loan.
