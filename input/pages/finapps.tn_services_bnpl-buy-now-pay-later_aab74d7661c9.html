BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later)

  1. Home

  2. BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later)

Empower Your Customers. Drive Sales. Simplify Payments.

In today’s consumer-driven economy, flexibility in payment methods can significantly impact purchasing behavior. At Finapps, our Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) solution is designed to give your customers the freedom to split payments into manageable installments — while ensuring full control, security, and integration for banks and merchants.

 

Our BNPL system is fully customizable and integrates seamlessly into your banking infrastructure or merchant application. From credit scoring and customer eligibility to monthly deductions and penalty management — we handle it all. Whether you are a bank looking to launch BNPL at scale or a business aiming to boost sales conversion, we have the tools and expertise to support your growth.
 

✅ Full BNPL application for end users
✅ Back-office module for banks and merchants
✅ Automatic wallet deductions with due date tracking
✅ Scoring engine integration and eligibility checks
✅ Regulatory compliance and secure operations
✅ Seamless API integration with core banking systems
✅ White-labeled or co-branded options
